lc-visualizer v1.0.0

First release on CTAN. lc-visualizer typesets terms of the untyped lambda
calculus with TikZ: abstractions, the variables they bind and the ranges of
their bodies are highlighted in matching colors. Terms can be reduced
automatically (alpha and eta conversions, beta reductions, user-defined
rewriting rules), every step of a reduction can be shown, and terms can be
typeset with de Bruijn indices.

https://github.com/EagleoutIce/latex-lambda-calculus-visualizer
